<Info>
  Every charge is metered and billed **weekly**. Check [Settings → Usage](https://www.noetive.io/settings/usage) to track spend before each weekly invoice.
</Info>

## Semantik

| Charge | Unit | What it covers |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Namespace | per namespace | Each active namespace, charged for the week |
| Requests | per request | Each publish, search, and subscribe call to Semantik |
| Storage | GB-hours | Message data retained, measured as gigabytes × hours stored |
| Embedding tokens | per token | Tokens embedded when you publish a message, run a search, or open a subscription |

Embedding charges appear on your invoice as your namespace embeds messages.

For current rates, see [noetive.io/pricing](https://www.noetive.io/pricing).

## Bud

You pay for the calls your agents make and the messages they send. There is no charge per mailbox, and mail your agents receive is free.

| Charge | Rate | What it covers |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Bud Requests | €0.01 per 1,000 requests | Each authenticated call to the Bud API. Opening a `watch` stream counts once, however long it stays open |
| Bud Messages Sent | €0.10 per 1,000 messages | Each message sent, counted once however many recipients it has, when Bud accepts it, whether or not it is delivered |

For example, one agent making 300,000 requests and sending 3,000 messages in a month costs €3.00 + €0.30 = €3.30.

<Note>
  Every authenticated request counts, including ones refused as invalid and calls to `health` and `catalog.describe`. Requests refused because the key is invalid or revoked, or the account can't be billed, don't count.
</Note>

Prices exclude VAT. New accounts get $30 in signup credits, used up before any card is charged. The rates are also on [noetive.io/bud/pricing](https://www.noetive.io/bud/pricing), which has a cost calculator.

## Subscription status

This section covers Semantik namespaces.

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Active | Billing is current; all features available |
| Pending | A payment is being processed |
| Cancelled | Subscription ended; namespace creation disabled |

If a payment fails, Noetive sends a reminder email three days after the missed payment. On cancellation, existing namespaces remain readable until the end of the billing period.

## Viewing invoices

Go to [Settings → Billing](https://www.noetive.io/settings/billing) to see your invoice history. Each row shows the invoice date, amount, status (paid, open, or void), and a PDF download link.

## Managing payment

Select **Manage billing** to open the Stripe customer portal where you can update your payment method, change your plan, or cancel.
