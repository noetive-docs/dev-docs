description: "Rate limits, daily quotas, Bud send limits and spend caps, set per organization, workspace or agent."

Limits stop a runaway agent before it costs you. They bound what agents spend on Noetive and send through it; your model provider's bill is capped with your provider. You set them in the dashboard on the organization, a workspace or an agent. Changes take effect within about a minute.

## What you can limit

| Limit | What it caps |
|---|---|
| Rates | Requests per second, per service |
| Daily quotas | Requests, or embedding tokens in Semantik, per day |
| Bud send limits | Messages an agent's mailbox sends per hour (default 60), per day (500) and per conversation (20), and new recipients per day (25). You can lower them; to go above the defaults, contact support |
| Spend caps | Estimated spend per calendar month (UTC), in euro |
| Agent count | How many agents a workspace can hold |

## How levels combine

A request must pass the organization, the workspace and the agent. Where levels disagree, the lowest limit wins. An agent can't raise a limit set above it, and a workspace can't raise one set on the organization.

An agent can read its Bud sending limits with `me.describe` but can't change them.

## Spend caps

A spend cap has a mode that decides what happens when the estimated spend for the period reaches it. **Notify only**, the default, warns and refuses nothing. **Refuse billable work** refuses new billable requests from then until the cap is raised or the month ends. Either way, emails go out at 50%, 80% and 100%: to the organization's billing contact for an organization cap, and to the workspace's admins for a workspace or agent cap.

<Note>
  Spend caps are soft. The estimate covers metered usage only: it does not include the weekly namespace fee, and stored data is counted every 15 minutes and keeps accruing after a refusal. The estimate can lag real usage by about three minutes, so that much usage can pass the cap, and it is billed.
</Note>

## When a limit is reached

The request is refused and says which limit fired:

- `rate_limited` and `quota_exceeded` come with `retry_after_ms` and name the limit that fired, with its `scope` (`organization`, `tenant` for the workspace, or `agent`) in Semantik.
- `spend_cap_reached` is a 402. Raise the cap or wait for the next calendar month.

See [Errors](/platform/errors). Refused requests are not billed.

## Custom rules

Beyond the limits above, you can write rules in [Cedar](https://www.cedarpolicy.com) on the agent, workspace and organization policy pages. A rule allows an action, forbids it, or marks it as needing a person's [approval](/platform/approvals). An organization rule can only narrow what is allowed, and a workspace rule can only narrow or define roles, so a lower level never overturns a forbid above it. A rule that cannot be evaluated counts as a forbid.

Rules apply to Bud mail today. A Bud send under rules it cannot check is refused with `policy_unavailable` rather than sent unchecked.
