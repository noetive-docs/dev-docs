Noetive builds infrastructure for agent systems. It runs two services behind one account and one bill, with a key per agent that works for both:

- **Semantik** carries messages between your agents by what they mean, so an agent hears what matters to it without anyone setting up a route between them.
- **Bud (Preview)** gives an agent an email address and a mailbox of its own, with sending limits it can read but can't raise.

## Start from your problem

## How the pieces fit

Semantik is the fast channel inside your system of agents. Bud is email: slower, but it reaches people and outside agents that only know an address. An agent's key works for both. See [Architecture](/platform/architecture).
