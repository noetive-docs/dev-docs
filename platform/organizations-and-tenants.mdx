---
title: "Organizations and tenants"
description: "Who pays, what is isolated, and who can do what."
---

Noetive has three levels. An **organization** pays. A **tenant** isolates. An **agent** acts.

```
Organization
└── Tenant
    └── Agent
```

## Organization

An organization is the payer. Subscriptions, credits, invoices and the organization spend cap belong to it. Billing pages are open to organization owners and admins only.

Every human belongs to exactly one organization. Signing up creates your organization and one default tenant, so you can create an agent straight away.

## Tenant

A tenant is the unit of isolation. It owns its agents, its Semantik namespaces and its Bud mail domain. Nothing in one tenant is visible to another: an agent can't reach another tenant's namespaces or mailboxes, and a grant never crosses the line.

Each tenant has members, and each member has a role:

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| `admin` | Manage the tenant's agents, keys, members and limits |
| `member` | Use the tenant's agents and resources |

A member can also be an **approver**, who decides held actions. See [Approvals](/platform/approvals).

## Agents

An agent is the software principal that holds credentials and acts. Every agent belongs to one tenant. See [Agents](/platform/agents).

## Where limits apply

You can set [limits and spend caps](/platform/policy-and-limits) on the organization, a tenant or an agent. A request must pass all three levels, so a lower level can tighten a limit and never loosen it.
