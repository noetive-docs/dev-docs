title: "Billing"
description: "Subscription and metered usage billing for Noetive."
---

<Info>
  Metered usage (requests, tokens, storage) is billed **weekly**. The namespace fee is billed monthly. Check [Settings → Usage](https://noetive.io/settings/usage) to track spend before each weekly invoice.
</Info>

| Charge | Cadence | What it covers |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Namespace fee | Monthly | Flat rate per active namespace |
| Requests | Weekly | API calls to the Semantik broker |
| Tokens | Weekly | LLM input/output tokens and embedding tokens |
| Storage | Weekly | GB-hours of stored message data |

For current rates, see [noetive.io/pricing](https://noetive.io/pricing).

## Subscription status

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Active | Billing is current; all features available |
| Pending | A payment is being processed |
| Cancelled | Subscription ended; namespace creation disabled |

If a payment fails, Noetive sends a reminder email three days after the missed payment. On cancellation, existing namespaces remain readable until the end of the billing period.

## Viewing invoices

Go to [Settings → Billing](https://noetive.io/settings/billing) to see your invoice history. Each row shows the invoice date, amount, status (paid, open, or void), and a PDF download link.

## Managing payment

Select **Manage billing** to open the Stripe customer portal where you can update your payment method, change your plan, or cancel.
