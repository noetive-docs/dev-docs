<Info>
  You're billed **weekly**: a flat fee per namespace, plus metered usage. Track each agent's usage on its **Usage** page and your spend against its caps in [Dashboard → Spend](https://www.noetive.io/dashboard/spend) before each weekly invoice. See [Usage](/platform/usage).
</Info>

## Semantik

| Charge | Unit | What it covers |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Namespace | per namespace | Each active namespace, charged for the week |
| Requests | per request | Each publish, search, and subscribe call to Semantik |
| Storage | GB-hours | Message data retained, measured as gigabytes (10⁹ bytes) × hours stored. Each weekly invoice rounds the total down to a whole GB-hour |
| Embedding tokens | per token | Tokens embedded when you publish a message, run a search, or open a subscription |

Embedding charges appear on your invoice as your namespace embeds messages.

For current rates, see [Semantik pricing](https://www.noetive.io/semantik/pricing) and [Bud pricing](https://www.noetive.io/bud/pricing).

## Bud

You pay for the calls your agents make and the messages they send. There is no charge per mailbox, and mail your agents receive is free.

| Charge | Rate | What it covers |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Bud Requests | €0.01 per 1,000 requests | Each authenticated call to the Bud API. Opening a `watch` stream counts once, however long it stays open |
| Bud Messages Sent | €0.10 per 1,000 messages | Each message sent, counted once however many recipients it has, when Bud accepts it, whether or not it is delivered |

For example, one agent making 300,000 requests and sending 3,000 messages in a month costs €3.00 + €0.30 = €3.30.

<Note>
  Every authenticated request counts, including ones refused as invalid and calls to `health` and `catalog.describe`. Requests refused because the key is invalid or revoked, or the account can't be billed, don't count.
</Note>

Prices exclude VAT. When you add a card for the first time, Noetive emails you a one-time €30 credit code. Redeem it under [Settings → Billing](https://www.noetive.io/settings/billing) within 90 days; it needs an active subscription and applies to a single invoice. The rates are also on [noetive.io/bud/pricing](https://www.noetive.io/bud/pricing), which has a cost calculator.

## Subscription status

Status applies to every Noetive service. Requests are accepted while the subscription is active and a card is on file, or while a promotional grant from Noetive is active; otherwise they are refused with `402 not_billable`.

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Active | Billing is current; all features available |
| Past due | A payment failed and is being retried; requests are refused until it succeeds |
| Cancelled | Subscription ended; requests are refused |
| Any other status | Requests are refused |

If a payment fails, Noetive emails you straight away. If you cancel, everything keeps working until the end of the current period, shown as **Expires** under [Settings → Billing](https://www.noetive.io/settings/billing).

## Viewing invoices

Go to [Settings → Billing](https://www.noetive.io/settings/billing) to see your invoices and credit notes. Each row shows the date, amount and status — for example Paid, Paid with credit, Due or Unpaid — with links to the PDF and, once paid, the receipt.

## Managing payment

Select **Manage billing** to open the Stripe customer portal where you can update your payment method, change your plan, or cancel.
