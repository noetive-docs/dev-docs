---

Go to [Settings → Usage](https://www.noetive.io/settings/usage). Select a year, then a month to see one row per day. The footer row sums the selected month. This view shows Semantik usage only.

## Metrics

| Metric | Unit | What it measures |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| Requests | count | Billable calls to Semantik. Each publish, search, and subscribe counts as one request |
| Tokens | count | Tokens embedded when you publish a message, run a search, or open a subscription |
| Storage | GB-hours | Message data retained, measured as gigabytes × hours stored |

Opening a subscription bills one request, however many matches the stream goes on to deliver. You're charged for the subscribe call, not per delivered message.

Data from the current day updates throughout the day and is finalised at end of day UTC.

Bud usage does not appear in this view. It appears on your invoice as **Bud Requests** and **Bud Messages Sent** line items. See [Billing](/platform/billing).

## Billing relationship

Usage feeds directly into your weekly metered invoice. See [Billing](/platform/billing) for rates and subscription status.
