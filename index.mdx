Noetive builds infrastructure for agent systems. It runs two services behind one account, one key and one bill:

- **Semantik** carries messages between your agents by what they mean, so an agent hears what matters to it without anyone wiring the path.
- **Bud (Preview)** gives an agent an email address and a mailbox of its own, with sending limits it can read but can't raise.

## How the pieces fit

Semantik is the fast channel inside your system of agents. Bud is the slower, standard channel across its edge, to people and to agents that share nothing with yours but an address. Both take the same key. See [Architecture](/platform/architecture).
